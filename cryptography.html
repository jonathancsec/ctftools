Auto-decode

Tries common encodings plus Caesar/ROT, Atbash, ROT47, rail fence, A1Z26, Bacon, Polybius, affine and Vigenère, and cracks Vigenère and Beaufort keys from letter frequencies. Results are ranked by how English-like they look, so short or non-English plaintext can be misleading. Columnar, Bifid and Playfair still need their keyword. Up to 5,000 characters per run.
